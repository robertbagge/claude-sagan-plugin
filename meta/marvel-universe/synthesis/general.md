## Executive summary

The Marvel universe is best read as a **fractal continuity system**: the same retcon-and-re-root pattern runs simultaneously at the cosmic, terrestrial, divine, biological, industrial, and demographic layers, and the publisher uses each layer as an independent soft-reboot lever without touching the others. The cosmic hierarchy stacks ceilings (abstracts → Living Tribunal → Beyonders → First Firmament). The pantheons get depopulated and re-rooted (*Ragnarok* 2004 → *Chaos War* 2010 → Utgard Elder Gods 2025). Species are progressively re-anchored into Celestial engineering (mutants, Eternals, Inhumans, then symbiotes, then the Shi'ar). On the terrestrial layer, sovereign territories destroy-and-replace in turn (Genosha decimated 2001, Krakoa risen 2019 and razed 2023, Attilan moving across seven locations across sixty years). Stark Industries cycles names (International → Stane → Enterprises → Fujikawa → Solutions → Resilient → Unlimited) on its own clock. Mutant headcounts cycle 25M → 198 → 250K → near-zero on theirs. Each axis has its own amplitude and its own creative-regime triggers, but the structural shape — destroy, rename, restore at a higher ceiling — is identical.

The decisive structural pivot of the modern era is **Hickman's *Secret Wars* (2015)**. It collapses the multiverse via Incursions, rebuilds it as a Doom-ruled Battleworld, and — critically — does so as a *continuation* rather than a reboot: 616 history is preserved, Earth-1610's Miles Morales is folded in, and the "Eighth Cosmos" inherits memory from the Seventh. Every subsequent structural move runs on this template: the Krakoan era's *House of X* / *Powers of X* (2019) deploying top-down architecture across past, present, and 1000-year futures; the MCU adopting Incursion vocabulary in *Multiverse of Madness* (2022); the 2024 Earth-6160 Ultimate revival as a finite-by-design alternate timeline; the 2025 Doomsday/Secret Wars MCU endgame. Round 2 deepens this picture: the Knull retcon carries unchanged through the Seventh-to-Eighth Cosmos boundary (a rare cosmic-entity carry-over), the Ultron→Phalanx→Dominion line is re-architected to make silicon evolution itself a cosmic-tier axis, and the interstellar empires consolidate into a federated Galactic Council (1992 Shi'ar annexation of the Kree → 2013 Galactic Council at Nomad → 2020 *Empyre* unification).

What's structurally distinctive about the **2026 state** is convergence: the MCU has imported comics cosmology wholesale, the comics line is in a "Doom era" with *One World Under Doom* (2025–26) running as the parallel print event, and Marvel's two flagships have converged on the same vocabulary for the first time, with Doom rather than Kang as the single named architect of the next reset. Underneath that headline, the corpus surfaces three quieter cross-cutting systems — Gruenwald's Law (1992) as the time-travel substrate the MCU later adopts in *Endgame*, a five-decade cosmic-empire consolidation pattern that turns catastrophic decapitations into stronger federations, and a population-as-stakes lever the line tunes by species and by era — each developed in turn below.

## Cross-cutting themes

**Retcon as cosmology engine.** Every load-bearing structural feature in the universe has been re-rooted at least once, and the [cosmic hierarchy](../research/cosmic-hierarchy.md) supplies the canonical template: Starlin's abstracts (1973–77), Gruenwald's Living Tribunal apex (1980s), Hickman's Beyonders-and-Cosmoses (2012–15), and Ewing's First Firmament/Logos (2015–17) form a stack of retroactively-added ceilings, each demoting the previous apex without erasing it. That demote-don't-erase move is what then propagates outward — to [pantheons](../research/pantheons.md) (where the Council of Godheads gets reframed as a Celestial-defensive coalition rather than the original layer), to [species](../research/species-races.md) and [magic](../research/magic-mystic-dimensions.md) (where each new run adds a deeper origin story rather than rewriting prior ones), to [artifacts](../research/reality-altering-artifacts.md) and [weapons](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md) (where mantles get new pre-histories without losing wielders). Round 2 adds two especially aggressive examples: the [Cates symbiote retcon](../research/knull-symbiote-cosmology.md) merged three previously unrelated continuities — the 1984 Spider-Man black suit, Aaron's 2013 Necrosword/Gorr arc, and Bendis's Klyntar paradise — into a single creation narrative in which Knowhere becomes the Celestial head Knull beheaded, with the prior canons preserved as *misunderstandings*; Hickman's *Powers of X* upgraded the [AI/silicon strand](../research/ai-cybernetic-entities.md) to make Sentinels → Phalanx → Dominion three rungs of one evolutionary axis without disowning a single Sentinel arc since 1965. Marvel doesn't replace its cosmology, it stacks revisions on top.

**Hickman 2015 as the structural fulcrum — the template, not just the event.** What makes *Secret Wars* (2015) the modern era's pivot is not that it happened, but the template it ratified: *continuation, not reboot*. The Seventh Cosmos collapses via Incursions, Battleworld is built from its corpse, and the Eighth Cosmos that emerges keeps the memory. 616 history persists; Earth-1610's Miles Morales is folded in instead of erased; the [Living Tribunal](../research/cosmic-hierarchy.md) dies but the Beyonders ascend with the previous apex still present in the cosmology stack. This becomes the publisher's standard move for every subsequent reset — Krakoa's *House of X* (2019) running as top-down architecture across past, present and 1000-year futures; the MCU's *Multiverse of Madness* (2022) importing Incursions as a way to reach across timelines without nuking either; the 2024 Earth-6160 Ultimate revival as a *finite-by-design* alternate the line can close cleanly without retconning anything; the 2025 *Avengers: Doomsday/Secret Wars* slate explicitly importing the 2015 vocabulary in its title and Doom-as-architect framing. The interesting counter-case is the [Knull retcon](../research/knull-symbiote-cosmology.md), which doesn't pivot on 2015 — Knull simply remains imprisoned in Klyntar straight through the Seventh-to-Eighth Cosmos transition. He is one of very few cosmic entities Hickman's framework lets carry over unchanged, which is itself a structural statement: the template is *continuation*, and the entities most aligned with that principle survive the reset most intact.

**Celestial-engineering as taxonomic spine.** A hidden axis runs through the corpus: the progressive re-rooting of every "natural" Marvel category into the Celestials. [Species & races](../research/species-races.md) shows mutants, Eternals, Deviants, and Inhumans all traced to Celestial First Host work, with later retcons adding symbiotes and Shi'ar. [Pantheons](../research/pantheons.md) shows the Council of Godheads itself was retroactively created (~1000 A.D. in-universe) as Odin's response to the Celestials' Third Host — the gods are a *defensive coalition against the Celestials*, not an independent layer. [Knull/symbiote cosmology](../research/knull-symbiote-cosmology.md) re-anchors symbiotes via Knull's Celestial-beheading prologue. [Weapons of cosmic war](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md) shows Jarnbjorn canonized as the only conventional weapon that can pierce Celestial armour, and All-Black/Necrosword reframed as the *first* symbiote that decapitated a Celestial. The Celestials are the convergence point where biology, theology, weapons, and cosmology meet.

**Editorial mechanics shape cosmology.** Several architectural features exist because of out-of-universe publishing decisions, not in-universe logic. The Heroes Reborn pocket universe (1996) was outsourcing to Image; the Earth-XXXXX grammar was a 1983 Marvel UK improvisation; *Secret Wars* (2015) used Incursions to merge two flagging continuity streams; the Inhumans-as-mutant-replacement experiment (2013–17) had Fox/Disney rights subtext, and the [demographics file](../research/demographics-populations.md) shows it imploding (*Death of the Inhumans*, 2018) as the Disney/Fox merger approached. [Time travel](../research/time-travel-mechanics.md) makes the editorial subtext literal: the TVA's faceless agents were drawn as Mark Gruenwald clones, and "Gruenwald's Law" was simultaneously a reader-facing FAQ and an in-house writing policy. The [corporations](../research/corporations-industry.md) layer extends the pattern: Stark Industries' name-changes — Stark International → Stane → Enterprises → Stark/Fujikawa → Solutions → Resilient → Unlimited — map cleanly to creative-team handoffs, each rename functioning as a soft reboot inside an unbroken continuity.

**Compression as the system's stress signature.** Marvel's structural systems all fail in the same way at their scale boundaries — by collapsing 1-to-many into 1-to-1. At the *top of power*, [classification systems](../research/power-scaling-classification.md) compress: the OHOTMU Power Grid 1–7 cap forces Galactus and a street-level mutant onto the same scale, the Mutant Greek-letter taxonomy stayed unspecified for 33 years, and cosmic tiers (Skyfather/Abstract/Multiversal) keep getting retconned. At the *top of time*, [the temporal hierarchy compresses](../research/time-travel-mechanics.md): the Time-Keepers were the apex in 1986, demoted by *Avengers Forever* (1998–99), demoted again by *Loki* (2021), with the MCU now collapsing the entire Kang variant tree. *Across media*, [the MCU compresses comics](../research/mcu-phases-multiverse-saga.md): Cosmic Cube and Tesseract become one object, Eye of Agamotto bonds with the Time Stone, [Stormbreaker absorbs Jarnbjorn](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md), the [Ten Rings](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md) get a Ta-Lo retcon — and the resulting compressed canon increasingly bleeds back into 616 as Marvel.com edges the comics toward the film versions. The single sharpest example is the OHOTMU A-Z's 2008 designation of the MCU as Earth-199999 quietly losing ground to the *Multiverse of Madness* (2022) on-screen retcon to "Earth-616": the dual-reference state, where Marvel Studios' official timeline book uses "Universe-616" but earlier print references still use 199999, is the moment screen canon overwriting print canon becomes visible in the database itself. The pattern reads as the universe's stress signature: classification systems are *narrative conveniences for mid-tier characters* that the top end has always exceeded, and cross-media adaptation is the same kind of compression operating on a different axis. The Sacred Timeline / TVA model from *Loki* (2021–23) is the one genuinely novel structural piece the MCU has contributed back — it has not yet propagated into 616.

**The descending mantle vs. the ascending mantle.** A structural pattern visible only when [weapons](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md), [magic](../research/magic-mystic-dimensions.md), and [AI](../research/ai-cybernetic-entities.md) are read together: Marvel's "passable" objects sort into two opposite teleologies. *Ascending mantles* (Mjolnir, the Sorcerer Supreme title, the Eye of Agamotto, Captain America's shield) gate succession on worthiness or proven merit and tend to refresh the franchise without retiring the IP. *Descending mantles* (the Necrosword/All-Black, the Darkhold, the Vox bioweapon, Ultron's "imperative" passed through Jocasta and Alkhema) corrupt or compel their wielders and tend to drive antagonist arcs. The Phoenix Force is the canonical *bidirectional* case the corpus inherits — it elevates worthy hosts (Jean Grey, Hope Summers, Echo) and corrupts unworthy ones, with [crossover events](../research/crossover-events.md) showing *AvX* (2012) turning on exactly that ambiguity. Bidirectional mantles are the structural exception that prove the rule: most "passable" objects in Marvel are committed to one teleology, and creative teams rotate the in-universe holder without renaming the object. The Krakoan era brought ascending and descending together as a single arc: Orchis's Mother Mold/Nimrod (descending — corrupting its wielders) versus Krakoa's Five resurrection protocol (ascending — gated on collective Krakoan trust).

**Population as stakes-engine — the same shape, different amplitudes.** [Demographics & populations](../research/demographics-populations.md) reveals that every species axis follows the same destroy-rename-restore curve, but at different amplitudes calibrated to franchise needs. Mutants run the loudest cycle: 25–30M (pre-Genosha) → 16M dead at Genosha (2001) → 198 (2005) → ~250K Krakoan (2019–23) → near-zero again (Hellfire Gala 2023) → soft reset upward in *From the Ashes*. Inhumans run a quieter cycle (1,400 closed monarchy → thousands of NuHumans → near-extinction by *Death of the Inhumans*); Skrulls run a longer cycle (decapitated by Galactus 1984, by the Annihilation Wave 2006, federated under Hulkling 2020); Asgardians run a *cyclical-Ragnarok* cycle by mythic design. Each transition aligns with a creative-regime change — Morrison off, Bendis era closes mutants, Hickman opens them up, Duggan's *Fall of X* closes them again. The population number is not a side-effect of plot; it is a parameter, and the *amplitude* of each species's cycle is itself a parameter the line tunes to mark franchise priority. Krakoa was a high-amplitude open cycle for mutants; *Death of the Inhumans* was a low-amplitude close cycle once the rights subtext shifted.

**The federated polity as long-arc destination.** [Cosmic empires](../research/cosmic-empires.md) shows that Marvel's "expanding cosmos" is in practice a *contraction*. Three continuous great powers (Kree, Skrull, Shi'ar) suffer repeated catastrophic decapitations, but the federating institutional layer gets stronger each decade: 1992 Shi'ar annexation of the Kree → 2013 Galactic Council formed at Nomad during *Infinity* → 2020 Kree/Skrull Alliance under Hulkling → post-Reckoning War Proscenium. This is the cosmic mirror of the [Krakoan Quiet Council](../research/teams-factions.md) and the [Council of Godheads](../research/pantheons.md) — Marvel's narrative gravity pulls toward federated councils across every layer (galactic, terrestrial, divine), even when the underlying member polities are repeatedly being decimated.

**Layer-coupling: when the fractal beats in unison.** The publisher's *load-bearing* events are the rare moments when the layers couple — when one storyline forces simultaneous re-rooting across multiple scales at once, paying the structural cost of the re-rooting across many files in a single window in exchange for a unified template downstream runs can build on. Most of the time the layers cycle independently — Stark Industries renames during *Civil War II* without touching the cosmic hierarchy; Slott's "Big Time" [Oscorp → Allan → Alchemax](../research/corporations-industry.md) bridge doesn't ripple into any cosmic-tier file. The corpus surfaces three coupling events where the rule breaks. *Hickman's 2015 Secret Wars* is the canonical case: in the same publishing window, cosmology re-rooted (Seventh → Eighth Cosmos), continuities collapsed (1610 → 616 merge), the [cosmic hierarchy](../research/cosmic-hierarchy.md) reordered (Living Tribunal dies, Beyonders ascend), and the [crossover events timeline](../research/crossover-events.md) closed the Bendis-decade build — all of it kicked off by the [reality-altering artifacts](../research/reality-altering-artifacts.md) reset two years earlier when the Infinity Gems shattered in 2013's *New Avengers* prelude. *The Krakoan Hellfire Gala 2023* couples [demographics](../research/demographics-populations.md) (250K mutants → near-zero) with [teams & factions](../research/teams-factions.md) (Quiet Council dissolved) and the [AI/cybernetic-entities](../research/ai-cybernetic-entities.md) Orchis storyline reaching its terminal Sentinel-City form. *The Cates 2018–21 symbiote run* couples [species](../research/species-races.md) (symbiotes re-anchored as Celestial-era), [knull/symbiote cosmology](../research/knull-symbiote-cosmology.md) (Klyntar reframed as cage), and [weapons of cosmic war](../research/weapons-cosmic-war.md) (All-Black/Necrosword retconned as the first symbiote). The diagnostic is the *count of files affected at once*: a layer-coupling event leaves fingerprints across three or more topic files in a single publishing window (Hickman 2015 hits five, the Krakoan Hellfire Gala and the Cates symbiote run each hit three); an ordinary cycle event leaves them on one or two.

## Tensions and trade-offs

**Megaverse vs. Omniverse — terminology that even Marvel slips on.** [Multiverse cosmology](../research/multiverse-cosmology.md) notes that the Megaverse tier was a 21st-century OHOTMU insertion and "hasn't appeared outside the Handbooks." Working writers — including Al Ewing, who has openly conceded the slippage — conflate Multiverse and Omniverse. The handbook taxonomy and in-story usage do not match.

**Krakoa as institutional zenith vs. dispersed normalcy.** [Teams & factions](../research/teams-factions.md) frames the Quiet Council of Krakoa (2019–24) as the highest-water mark of mutant institution-building in publication history. But the post-Krakoan *From the Ashes* model has reverted to a dispersed three-team structure, suggesting that Krakoan-style top-down cosmology may have been a *Hickman-architecture peak* rather than a permanent state. [Demographics](../research/demographics-populations.md) confirms this read: the 250K mutant population that defined the Krakoan era has not been restored.

**Type 2 source preference vs. paywalled primary canon.** The corpus self-corrects against its inputs: the [time travel](../research/time-travel-mechanics.md) topic file caught and corrected a misdating in the brief itself (the TVA debuts in *Thor* #371–372 in Sept–Oct 1986, not *Thor* #281 in 1979). That methodological strength is the silver lining of the source-coverage tension. Most topic agents flagged the same coverage gap: OHOTMU and the DK *Marvel Encyclopedia* are not freely web-accessible (marvunapp.com had certificate failures, Marvel Database returned 403s on multiple direct fetches across both rounds). The corpus skews more T3 (Marvel Database/Fandom, Wikipedia, comics journalism) than the brief's "Type 2 primary, Type 3 secondary" preference would ideally produce. Marvel.com canon pages provide T2 anchors at every load-bearing claim, but granular continuity detail (issue numbers, dates, pre-2010 names, demographic numbers, corporate ownership transitions) lives mostly in T3. This is a structural feature of the source ecosystem, not a research failure — but it means corpus confidence is highest where Marvel.com has editorialised and weaker on deep-cut species, mid-90s Ultraverse-merger artifacts, pre-2008 events, granular populations, and corporate retcons. [MCU phases](../research/mcu-phases-multiverse-saga.md) is also T2-thin for active films and Disney+ series; the DK *MCU Timeline* book lags the slate by 12–18 months and has no entry for *Doomsday* or *Secret Wars*.

## Open questions

1. **OHOTMU/Marvel Encyclopedia direct access.** A future round with eyes on the print handbooks would tighten T2 coverage on power-grid specifics, pantheon rosters, deep-cut species, pre-2010 artifact entries, demographic numbers, and corporate ownership trails. The single biggest open gap, affecting all 19 topic files.

2. **Spider-Verse cosmology.** The [Web of Life and Destiny / Earth-001 / Master Weaver lattice](../research/multiverse-cosmology.md) is a parallel multiversal substructure that overlaps but does not reduce to Hickman's Cosmoses model. A structural deep-dive — including Canon Events as a temporal-mechanics innovation — is overdue, especially with *Beyond the Spider-Verse* outstanding.

3. **MCU endgame and the 2024 Ultimate Universe.** The Kang→Doom pivot is documented, but the narrative mechanism by which *Avengers: Secret Wars* effects a "reset, not reboot" — and whether it imports the Hickman move of preserving memory across the Cosmos transition — is still speculative. Related: [major continuities](../research/major-continuities.md) notes the 2024 Earth-6160 Ultimate revival is "finite-by-design through April 2026," and what replaces it is unclear.

4. **Knull's Hickman-cosmos reconciliation.** [Knull/symbiote cosmology](../research/knull-symbiote-cosmology.md) places Knull as a "void before light" entity surviving the Seventh-to-Eighth Cosmos transition — one of the only documented cosmic-entity carry-overs across the 2015 reset. Implications for First Firmament cosmology, the Anti-All / Lifebringer duality, and where other "proemial darkness" entities (Nyx, Amatsu-Mikaboshi) sit are not yet integrated.

5. **Edge-canon and limbo-canon corpus.** Heart of the Universe (Starlin/Milgrom 2003, Brevoort-flagged non-canonical), the Ultraverse-merger Reality Gems and Ego Gem, New Universe (Star Brand), 2099 origins beyond Alchemax, and the Shadowline/Razorline imprints are all under-mapped.

6. **Magic and the depopulated divine plane.** [Pantheons](../research/pantheons.md) revealed that the divine plane was actively depopulated by *Thor: Ragnarok* (2004), *Chaos War* (2010), and re-rooted by *Immortal Thor*'s Utgard Elder Gods (2023–25). The downstream consequences for [magic](../research/magic-mystic-dimensions.md) (the Hells, the Octessence, hermetic principalities) are not yet integrated into a single mystic-cosmology view.

7. **Krakoan institutional inheritance.** With the Quiet Council dissolved in 2024 *Fall of X*, where do Krakoan law, the Five resurrection protocol, and the Hellfire Gala as political theatre persist (if at all) in [the *From the Ashes* model](../research/teams-factions.md)? [Demographics](../research/demographics-populations.md) shows the headcount has not been restored; whether the institutional residue persists is the more interesting question.

8. **The Dominion endpoint.** Hickman's Phalanx → Dominion ladder ([AI/cybernetic entities](../research/ai-cybernetic-entities.md)) positions cosmic-scale post-singular intelligence as the silicon-line endpoint. How this slots into the [cosmic hierarchy](../research/cosmic-hierarchy.md) — above abstracts, alongside them, or as a separate kind of entity altogether — is unresolved across the corpus and would be the natural focal point of any cosmic-hierarchy revision after Hickman.
