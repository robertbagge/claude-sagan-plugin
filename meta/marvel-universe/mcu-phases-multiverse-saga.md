# MCU Phases & the Multiverse Saga: A Structural Map

The Marvel Cinematic Universe is now an unusually well-documented continuity in three structural acts: an **Infinity Saga** (Phases 1–3) organised around the Infinity Stones and a single shared timeline, an in-progress **Multiverse Saga** (Phases 4–6) organised around branching realities, the Sacred Timeline, Incursions and a multi-villain replacement, and a planned reset/synthesis at *Avengers: Secret Wars* (December 2027). What follows is a structural — not biographical — map of that arc, focused on cosmology and institutional plumbing rather than character drama.

## 1. The Infinity Saga (Phases 1–3): a single shared timeline

The Infinity Saga ran from *Iron Man* (May 2008) to *Spider-Man: Far From Home* (July 2019), 23 films over three phases, all set in a single shared continuity centred on the six Infinity Stones [T3] Wikipedia, "List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films.

- **Phase One (2008–2012)** — *Iron Man*, *The Incredible Hulk*, *Iron Man 2*, *Thor*, *Captain America: The First Avenger*, *The Avengers*. Built individual franchises that converged in a crossover film [T3] Wikipedia, "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase One," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_One.
- **Phase Two (2013–2015)** — *Iron Man 3*, *Thor: The Dark World*, *Captain America: The Winter Soldier*, *Guardians of the Galaxy*, *Avengers: Age of Ultron*, *Ant-Man*. Expanded cosmic and espionage layers and seeded the Stones [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU films," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films.
- **Phase Three (2016–2019)** — *Captain America: Civil War* through *Spider-Man: Far From Home*, with *Infinity War* and *Endgame* as the structural climax [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU films," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films.

Cosmologically the Infinity Saga is **monoversal**: a single timeline, treated as the "real" world, in which time travel in *Endgame* is later retconned (in *Loki*) into branch creation rather than retroactive change. The reality-designation question — Earth-199999 vs. Earth-616 — is the cleanest marker of the structural shift between sagas: the 2008 *Official Handbook of the Marvel Universe* assigned the MCU Earth-199999, but *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* (2022) and the *Marvel Cinematic Universe: An Official Timeline* book (2023) retroactively brand the MCU prime as Earth-616 [T3] Gizmodo, "MCU Timeline Earth-616 vs Earth-199999 Confirmed," https://gizmodo.com/mcu-timeline-earth-616-vs-earth-199999-confirmed-marvel-1850972538; [T2] *Marvel Cinematic Universe: An Official Timeline* (DK, 2023), Miss Minutes intro: "the universe known as 616, also known as the Sacred Timeline" (cited in [T3] ScreenRant, "Earth-616 or 199999?," https://screenrant.com/earth-616-or-19999-the-mcus-universe-number-finally-has-an-official-answer/). *Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024) on-screen text confirms 616 [T3] ComicBookMovie, "Earth-616 or Earth-199999?," https://comicbookmovie.com/marvel-studios/earth-616-or-earth-199999-the-marvel-cinematic-universe-finally-has-an-official-designation-a207533.

The numbering swap is structurally meaningful: it pulls the MCU into the comics' multiversal lattice rather than treating it as a side reality, and it makes the 2008 OHOTMU designation obsolete — exactly the kind of retcon that this research project is mapping.

## 2. The hinge: Disney+ canon and the multiverse turn

Phase Four did not begin with a film but with the WandaVision premiere on Disney+ (15 January 2021), and the streaming series became the load-bearing infrastructure for the multiverse pivot [T3] Wikipedia, "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Four," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Four.

The Phase 4 Disney+ slate, in canon order: *WandaVision*, *The Falcon and the Winter Soldier*, *Loki* S1, *What If…?* S1, *Hawkeye*, *Moon Knight*, *Ms. Marvel*, *I Am Groot*, *She-Hulk: Attorney at Law*, plus the *Werewolf by Night* and *Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday* specials [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU television series (Marvel Studios)," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_television_series_(Marvel_Studios). Disney+'s in-house chronological ordering then reshuffles release order — placing *Loki* S1 and *What If…?* S1 between *Endgame* and *WandaVision*, and their respective second seasons after *The Marvels* — which signals that streaming canon is now timeline-canonical, not merely supplemental [T3] The Direct, "Disney+ Reveals New MCU Timeline Order," https://thedirect.com/article/mcu-marvel-cinematic-universe-timeline-order-disney-plus-loki.

Three series do specific structural work:

- **WandaVision** introduces chaos magic at multiversal scale and seeds the Darkhold; its mid-credits sting feeds directly into *Multiverse of Madness*.
- **Loki S1** introduces the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)**, **Sacred Timeline**, **variants**, **pruning**, **Alioth**, **He Who Remains**, and the **Multiversal War** — the entire multiverse vocabulary the saga runs on [T3] MCU Wiki, "Time Variance Authority," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Variance_Authority.
- **What If…?** demonstrates that branched realities have their own narrative weight, observed by the Watcher.

Other series are integrated rather than structurally pivotal: *Ms. Marvel* introduces Kamala Khan and ties into *The Marvels* (2023); *Secret Invasion* (2023), *Echo* (2024), *Agatha All Along* (2024), *Daredevil: Born Again* (2025) and *Ironheart* (2025) follow under Phase 5, alongside the Spider-Man animated series *Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man* and *Marvel Zombies* [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU television series," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_television_series_(Marvel_Studios).

## 3. Multiverse mechanics, by source of truth

The Multiverse Saga's rules are introduced piecewise across films and shows. Synthesised:

**The Sacred Timeline.** Per *Loki* S1, after a Multiversal War between his variants, the scientist later named **He Who Remains** weaponised **Alioth** and isolated a bundle of closely aligned timelines — the Sacred Timeline — which all converge on his birth and prevent the rise of evil Kang variants [T3] MCU Wiki, "Sacred Timeline," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Sacred_Timeline. Director Kate Herron has confirmed the Sacred Timeline is itself "an infinite number of unique universes," not a single line — which is the canon mechanism that lets *No Way Home*, *Multiverse of Madness*, and animated What If? branches all coexist within it [T3] Inverse, "Loki time travel reveals a surprising scientific truth about the multiverse," https://www.inverse.com/science/loki-branching-universes-time-travel.

**The TVA.** A bureaucratic agency outside time, created by He Who Remains, that detects branches off the Sacred path, prunes variants, and erases divergent branches. Its workers were themselves variants whose memories were wiped — a reveal in *Loki* S1 that destabilises the agency's legitimacy [T3] MCU Wiki, "Time Variance Authority," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Time_Variance_Authority.

**Branching and variants.** When a being makes a choice that deviates from the script, a branch forms; the deviant individual is a "variant." Pruning sends the variant to **the Void** at the End of Time, where Alioth consumes the doomed branch [T3] MCU Wiki, "Sacred Timeline," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Sacred_Timeline.

**Sylvie's break and the Kang return.** Sylvie kills He Who Remains in *Loki* S1's finale; the Sacred Timeline ruptures into uncontrolled branches, and dormant Kang variants begin returning [T3] CBR, "MCU Theory: Sacred Timeline Was Separated From the Multiverse," https://www.cbr.com/mcu-theory-sacred-timeline-separate-multiverse/.

**The Temporal Loom and Yggdrasil.** *Loki* S2 reveals the Loom as a failsafe to weave branches back into the Sacred Timeline; it cannot handle the post-Sylvie branch growth and tears itself apart. In the finale, Loki refuses both He Who Remains' choices, manually destroys the Loom, and reconstitutes the multiverse as **Yggdrasil**, a Norse Tree of Life with a trunk Loki himself sustains so every branch can grow with free will [T3] Nerdist, "What Happened in Loki Season 2's Finale," https://nerdist.com/article/loki-season-two-finale-explained-what-happened-to-mcu-timelines-yggdrasil-he-who-remains/; [T3] The Cosmic Circus, "Loki S2 Finale: Yggdrasil and the New MCU Timeline," https://thecosmiccircus.com/loki-season-2-finale-yggdrasil-and-the-new-mcu-timeline/. This is the most consequential cosmological retcon in the saga: the multiverse is no longer policed; branches are real, persistent, and free.

**Incursions.** Introduced in *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* (2022) by the Earth-838 Illuminati: an Incursion is a collision between two universes, typically caused by prolonged multiversal travel or Darkhold dreamwalking, that destroys one or both [T3] MCU Wiki, "Incursion," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Incursion; [T3] MovieWeb, "What is an Incursion in Doctor Strange and The Marvels?," https://movieweb.com/doctor-strange-in-the-multiverse-of-madness-incursion/. *Quantumania* (2023) extends this: Kang the Conqueror tells Janet van Dyne that the Multiversal War between his variants caused countless Incursions, motivating his exile [T3] ComicBook.com, "Marvel Theory: Council of Kangs," https://comicbook.com/marvel/news/marvel-cinematic-universe-timeine-theory-council-kangs-mcu-phase-4-5-movie-tv-shows/. Incursions are the structural payload that loads *Secret Wars*: they require a finite, depleting set of universes that must collide somewhere.

**Anchor Beings.** *Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024) adds the concept that some beings are load-bearing for their reality — when the anchor dies, the universe withers. Logan (the *Logan* (2017) Wolverine) is named the Fox-X-Men universe's anchor, and his death is what destabilised that branch [T3] Nerdist, "Deadpool and Wolverine TVA MCU Future Anchor Being," https://nerdist.com/article/deadpool-and-wolverine-tva-mcu-future-anchor-being/. The film also relocates the **Void** and the TVA's pruning machinery — a TVA "Time Ripper" can unmake whole realities — and introduces **Cassandra Nova** running the Void as a fiefdom [T3] MCU Wiki, "Cassandra Nova," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Cassandra_Nova.

The net effect: by mid-Phase 5 the multiverse has formal mechanics (branching, pruning, Incursions, anchors), formal infrastructure (TVA, Loom→Yggdrasil, Void), and a formal hostile force whose identity has changed mid-saga.

## 4. Phase 4 (2021–2022): introducing the multiverse

Films: *Black Widow* (2021), *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings* (2021), *Eternals* (2021), *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021), *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* (2022), *Thor: Love and Thunder* (2022), *Black Panther: Wakanda Forever* (2022) [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU films," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films.

Structural moves:

- **Eternals** retcons MCU cosmology backward: the Eternals and Celestials have been on Earth for millennia; Tiamut's emergence is averted, leaving a partial Celestial corpse in the Indian Ocean.
- **No Way Home** uses Strange's botched memory spell as a multiverse opener, importing villains and Spider-Men from prior film franchises (Sam Raimi's Maguire trilogy, Marc Webb's Garfield duology) [T3] Wikipedia, "Spider-Man: No Way Home," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spider-Man:_No_Way_Home. This is the first cinematic confirmation that prior non-MCU Spider-Man films sit somewhere in the now-canonical MCU multiverse.
- **Multiverse of Madness** is the first MCU film built around explicit multiversal travel, introduces America Chavez, the Illuminati of Earth-838, the Darkhold, dream-walking, and Incursions. Mid-credits sting introduces Clea (Charlize Theron) to address the Incursion Strange has caused [T3] ScreenRant, "What Are Incursions? Marvel's New Multiverse Problem Explained," https://screenrant.com/doctor-strange-2-incursions-mcu-marvel-multiverse-explained/.

Phase 4 also handles a generational handover: T'Challa exits (Boseman's death is written into *Wakanda Forever*), Hawkeye/Black Widow are succeeded (Yelena, Kate Bishop), and the X-side is teased via Patrick Stewart's Earth-838 Xavier and Reed Richards (Krasinski) cameos in *MoM*.

## 5. Phase 5 (2023–2025): the Kang plan and its collapse

Phase 5 was originally architected around **Kang the Conqueror**. *Quantumania* (2023) introduced him as Phase 5's Thanos-replacement, exiled by the **Council of Kangs**, with a post-credits Council scene and a *Loki* S2 sting featuring **Victor Timely** (a 19th-century Kang variant) [T3] Nerdist, "How Many Kang Variants Have We Seen in the MCU So Far?," https://nerdist.com/article/kang-variants-mcu-so-far-marvel-cinematic-universe-jonathan-majors-loki-quantumania/; [T3] MCU Wiki, "Council of Kangs," https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Council_of_Kangs. The Council is described as roughly 19 million Variants of Victor Timely, led by Immortus, with Rama-Tut and Scarlet Centurion among the named figureheads.

This plan collapses. After Jonathan Majors' December 2023 conviction for reckless assault and second-degree harassment, Marvel Studios dismissed him and pivoted away from Kang as the saga's central villain [T3] Yahoo Entertainment / The Wrap, "Why Was Jonathan Majors Fired as Kang by Marvel?," https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/why-jonathan-majors-fired-kang-041559209.html. At San Diego Comic-Con on 27 July 2024, Kevin Feige announced *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty* had been retitled and recentred as **Avengers: Doomsday**, with **Robert Downey Jr.** cast as **Doctor Doom** and the Russo Brothers returning to direct both *Doomsday* and *Secret Wars* [T3] ComingSoon, "Did Avengers: The Kang Dynasty Get Cancelled?," https://www.comingsoon.net/guides/features/1815739-avengers-the-kang-dynasty-5-doomsday-doctor-doom-jonathan-majors; [T3] Empire, "Robert Downey Jr. To Play Doctor Doom," https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/robert-downey-jr-to-play-doctor-doom-avengers-doomsday-and-secret-wars-russo-brothers/.

Films: *Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania* (2023), *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3* (2023), *The Marvels* (2023), *Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024), *Captain America: Brave New World* (2025), *Thunderbolts*\* (2025) [T3] Wikipedia, "List of MCU films," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films.

Structural payloads:

- *The Marvels* makes Incursions practical: a Kree weapon punches a hole between universes near Earth.
- *Deadpool & Wolverine* officially imports the **Fox-X-Men franchise** as a now-pruned MCU branch via the Void, sets up the Anchor-Being concept, and visually confirms the MCU prime as Earth-616 on screen [T3] ComicBookMovie, "Earth-616 or 199999?," https://comicbookmovie.com/marvel-studios/earth-616-or-earth-199999-the-marvel-cinematic-universe-finally-has-an-official-designation-a207533.
- *Thunderbolts*\* repositions a B-list ensemble as the New Avengers, filling the team-vacuum heading into *Doomsday*.

## 6. Phase 6 and the end-state: Doomsday, Secret Wars, Earth-828

Announced Phase 6 slate: *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* (2025), *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* (2026), *Avengers: Doomsday* (18 December 2026), *Avengers: Secret Wars* (17 December 2027) [T3] Wikipedia, "Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Six," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe:_Phase_Six; [T3] Wikipedia, "Avengers: Doomsday," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Doomsday.

**Earth-828.** *First Steps* is set on **Earth-828**, a retro-futuristic 1960s reality where the Fantastic Four are the only superheroes — a deliberate carve-out so the team's public-figure status doesn't have to be retconned into the Infinity Saga's history [T3] Time, "How Does The Fantastic Four: First Steps Connect to the MCU?," https://time.com/7304793/the-fantastic-four-first-steps-end-credits-scene-mcu/; [T3] Collider, "Earth-828 Explained: A Tribute to Jack Kirby," https://collider.com/the-fantastic-four-first-steps-earth-828-explained-mcu-jack-kirby/. The number is an homage to Jack Kirby's birthday (28 August). A title card promises the team will return in *Doomsday* — the structural mechanism for porting them to Earth-616 is not yet revealed but is presumed to be Incursion-driven.

**Avengers: Doomsday (2026).** Filmed at Pinewood from April–September 2025; Russo Brothers directing, Markus & McFeely writing, Silvestri scoring [T3] Wikipedia, "Avengers: Doomsday," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers:_Doomsday. Cast announced at the 27 March 2025 livestream is structurally significant, not just star-studded:

- Earth-616 incumbents: Hiddleston (Loki), Hemsworth (Thor), Mackie (Sam Wilson Cap), Rudd (Ant-Man), Stan (Bucky), Wright (Shuri), Huerta (Namor), Liu (Shang-Chi).
- Thunderbolts/New Avengers: Pugh (Yelena), Harbour (Red Guardian), Russell (US Agent), Pullman (Sentry), Ramirez (Falcon), John-Kamen (Ghost).
- Fantastic Four: Pascal (Reed), Kirby (Sue), Quinn (Johnny), Moss-Bachrach (Ben).
- Fox X-Men: Patrick Stewart (Xavier), Ian McKellen (Magneto), Alan Cumming (Nightcrawler), Rebecca Romijn (Mystique), James Marsden (Cyclops), Channing Tatum (Gambit), Kelsey Grammer (Beast).
- Returning anchors: Chris Evans (as Steve Rogers, per multiple outlets) [T3] ComicBookMovie, "Avengers: Doomsday Full Cast List Revealed," https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-doomsday/avengers-doomsday-full-cast-list-revealed-as-robert-downey-jr-doctor-dooms-theme-close-out-event-a217289; [T3] Empire, "Robert Downey Jr. To Play Doctor Doom," https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/robert-downey-jr-to-play-doctor-doom-avengers-doomsday-and-secret-wars-russo-brothers/.

The cast's structural shape is itself the story: *Doomsday* is being staged as a multi-reality convergence — Earth-616 Avengers + Earth-828 Fantastic Four + the pruned Fox-X branch + Thunderbolts replacements — against a Doom whose face is Tony Stark's, with Doom positioned as the saga's accumulated-Incursion antagonist.

**Avengers: Secret Wars (2027).** Set 17 December 2027. Marvel has signalled this as the saga's reset point, in line with the comics' 2015 *Secret Wars*, where surviving fragments of collapsing universes are stitched into a Battleworld. Outlets and theory pieces increasingly speculate Earth-828 will become the post-*Secret Wars* main reality, supplanting Earth-616 as the prime [T3] MovieWeb, "Earth-828 Should Be the MCU's Main Setting After Avengers: Secret Wars," https://movieweb.com/mcu-reboot-theory-earth-828-replacing-earth-616/. This is speculation, not announced canon, but it is the cleanest explanation for why Marvel built a separate-numbered-Earth Fantastic Four and is now converging both onto *Doomsday*.

## 7. What the Multiverse Saga has actually retconned

Stepping back, the Phase 4–6 arc is best understood as a series of structural retcons layered on the Infinity Saga rather than a clean continuation:

- **Time travel rules.** *Endgame*'s "you can't change your own past, only branch a new one" was a half-rule. *Loki* S1 retroactively recasts those branches as TVA pruning targets — meaning the post-*Endgame* Steve Rogers timeline, the snap-undo mechanics, and many "alternate" moments are now Sacred-Timeline-adjacent branches kept alive only because Loki preserved them.
- **Universe designation.** Earth-199999 (OHOTMU 2008) → Earth-616 (DK *Official Timeline* 2023, *Deadpool & Wolverine* 2024). The MCU prime now collides nominally with the comics prime, even though their internal histories don't match — a deliberate brand simplification that fan critics like Iman Vellani have publicly resisted [T3] ScreenRant, "Earth-616 or 199999?," https://screenrant.com/earth-616-or-19999-the-mcus-universe-number-finally-has-an-official-answer/.
- **The Big Bad.** Kang the Conqueror was set up across *Loki*, *Quantumania*, and *Loki* S2 to be Phase 6's Thanos. After Majors' firing the role has been transplanted onto Doctor Doom, with *Doomsday* replacing *The Kang Dynasty* and Kang materially excised from saga marketing [T3] GamesRadar, "Avengers 5 will no longer be called The Kang Dynasty," https://www.gamesradar.com/avengers-5-new-title-kang-dynasty-jonathan-majors/.
- **Multiverse infrastructure.** Sacred Timeline (S1) → ruptured branches (S1 finale) → Temporal Loom failure (S2) → Yggdrasil with Loki as living trunk (S2 finale). Each step changes who, if anyone, is policing the multiverse.
- **Cross-franchise canonisation.** Pre-MCU Spider-Man franchises (Raimi, Webb) and the Fox X-Men franchise are now formally part of the MCU multiverse via *No Way Home*, *Multiverse of Madness*, and *Deadpool & Wolverine* — a retroactive expansion of the canon footprint that the Infinity Saga did not need.

What sits unresolved heading into *Doomsday* (December 2026) is the basic question of which realities survive *Secret Wars* and what the post-2027 MCU's prime universe is called. The structural bet is a Battleworld-style synthesis that lets Marvel reset legacy continuity without admitting a reboot — the exact move that the comics' multiverse tooling (Incursions, Battleworld, Secret Wars) was historically designed to enable.
